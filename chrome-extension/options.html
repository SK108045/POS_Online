Field selectors

The eCitizen Vue customer dropdown, phone input, item popup and payment dropdown have default mappings. Override only controls verified by inspecting your live form. The extension never enters a customer name, changes tax classification, or clicks Create Invoice while filling.

Supported keys: pin, phone, email, billing_address, reference, note, payment_method, discount_type, discount_value, rows, add_item, item_modal, item_submit, item_name, item_description, item_quantity, item_price, total, seller_pin, generate, cu_invoice_number, scu_id, receipt_signature, invoice_number, invoice_date, invoice_root, qr.
